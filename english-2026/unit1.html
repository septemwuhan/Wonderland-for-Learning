本单元学习地图

教材依据：依据上海教育出版社七年级上册 Unit 1 Friendship 的栏目与词汇表整理。教材板块包括阅读、听力、Wh-questions、口语、语音、写作及拓展阅读。网页中的讲解和练习为辅助教学改编，不复制教材整篇课文。

学习目标

  • 能谈论自己和新朋友喜欢做的事情。
  • 能从外貌、兴趣和个人品质等方面描述朋友。
  • 能使用特殊疑问句获取人物信息。
  • 能表达自己对真正友谊的理解，并写一篇介绍朋友的帖子。

建议学习顺序

词汇理解 → 特殊疑问句 → 口语描述 → 阅读理解 → 语音辨析 → 写作输出 → 单元检测。每次学习15—25分钟，完成一个小模块即可。

本单元重点短语

表达含义例句

构词法：名词后缀 -er

教材提示：部分动词后加 -er 可以构成表示“做某事的人”的名词。

play → player（玩的人；运动员）
sing → singer（歌手）
teach → teacher（教师）
work → worker（工人）

注意：不是所有以 -er 结尾的词都能简单按此规则分析；本节重点是识别“动词 + -er → 执行动作的人”。

VOCABULARY PRACTICE

语法讲堂：Wh-questions

本单元核心语法：用特殊疑问句询问信息。

1. 特殊疑问词及其功能

疑问词询问内容例句
Who人物；谁Who is your best friend?
What事物、信息；什么What does he like doing?
Where地点；哪里Where does she live?
When时间；何时When is your friend's birthday?
Why原因；为什么Why do you like him?
Which选择；哪一个Which hobby do you share?
Whose所属关系；谁的Whose book is this?
How方式、状态；怎样How do you get to school?

2. 结构一：疑问词 + be动词 + 主语……？

Wh-word + am / is / are + 主语 + 其他成分?

用于句子中需要使用 be 动词的情况。注意 be 动词要根据主语选择 am、is 或 are。

  • Where are you now? — I’m at home.
  • How is your new school? — It’s great.
  • Who is your new friend? — Li Chen.
  • When is your birthday? — It’s in May.

3. 结构二：疑问词 + do / does + 主语 + 动词原形……？

Wh-word + do / does + 主语 + 动词原形 + 其他成分?

当句子谓语是一般现在时的行为动词时，使用 do 或 does 帮助构成疑问句。

  • What do you like doing? — I like swimming.
  • Where does she live? — She lives near our school.
  • Why does he like music? — Because it makes him happy.
  • What does your friend do after school? — He plays basketball.
关键：does 已经承担了第三人称单数标记，后面的行为动词必须用原形。正确：What does he like? 错误：What does he likes?

4. 特殊情况：疑问词本身作主语

当 who / what 本身就是句子的主语时，通常不再使用 do / does 倒装。

Who helps you with maths?（谁帮助你学数学？）
Who is your best friend?（谁是你最好的朋友？）

对比：Who do you help?（你帮助谁？）这里 you 才是主语，who 是宾语，因此需要 do。

5. 易错点对照

错误正确原因
Where you are?Where are you?be动词要放在主语前。
What does he likes?What does he like?does后用动词原形。
Where do she live?Where does she live?第三人称单数用does。
Why you like him?Why do you like him?行为动词疑问句需要助动词do。

6. 语法练习

句型与表达

从谈论兴趣到描述朋友，再完成一篇简短的朋友介绍。

1. 谈论兴趣爱好

What do you like doing? — I like swimming.
What does your friend like doing? — She likes painting pictures.
We share the same hobbies.（我们有相同的爱好。）

常见活动表达：ride a bicycle（骑自行车）、swim（游泳）、play the guitar（弹吉他）、paint pictures（画画）、read books（读书）、play basketball（打篮球）、play football（踢足球）、do crossword puzzles（做纵横字谜）。

2. 描述外貌

Body 身材

tall 高的
short 矮的
of medium height 中等身高

Face / Hair / Eyes

round / square face 圆脸 / 方脸
long / short hair 长发 / 短发
straight / curly hair 直发 / 卷发
dark / brown / blue eyes 深色 / 棕色 / 蓝色眼睛

She is tall. She has long, straight hair and big, dark eyes.

3. 描述个人品质

friendly 友好的hard-working 勤奋的helpful 乐于助人的honest 诚实的patient 有耐心的caring 体贴的kind 善良的polite 有礼貌的funny 有趣的supportive 支持他人的understanding 善解人意的

He is helpful and patient.（他乐于助人且有耐心。）
She is kind and caring.（她善良又体贴。）

4. 介绍朋友的常用句型

  • My best friend is ...（我最好的朋友是……）
  • He / She is ... years old.（他 / 她……岁。）
  • He / She is tall / of medium height.（他 / 她个子高 / 中等身高。）
  • He / She has ... hair and ... eyes.（他 / 她有……头发和……眼睛。）
  • He / She likes ...（他 / 她喜欢……）
  • He / She is good at ...（他 / 她擅长……）
  • When I need help, I can turn to him / her.（我需要帮助时可以向他 / 她求助。）
  • I can count on him / her.（我可以信赖他 / 她。）

5. 写作：Writing a post about your friend

建议按四个部分组织信息：

  1. Basic information：姓名、年龄、班级等。
  2. Appearance：身高、脸型、头发、眼睛等。
  3. Hobbies：喜欢做什么、擅长什么。
  4. Personal qualities：性格品质，并用具体事例说明。
My best friend
My best friend is Alex. He is twelve years old. He is of medium height and has short, dark hair. He likes playing basketball and reading stories. He is kind and helpful. When I have difficulty with maths, he explains the problems to me patiently. We often play basketball after school. I can trust him, and I am glad to have such a good friend.

写作自查：是否包含基本信息、外貌、爱好、品质？是否至少使用两个本单元短语？句首大写、句末标点是否正确？

6. 口语任务：采访你的同学

先征得对方同意，再选择以下问题进行采访。

  • May I ask you some questions? — Sure. Please go ahead.
  • How do you get to school?
  • What is your favourite film / song / book?
  • Why do you like it?
  • What do you want to do in the future?

任务：记录对方的三条信息，用英语向家长介绍这位同学。

阅读理解

训练段落主旨、细节定位、代词指代和主题理解。

READING 1 · ORIGINAL PRACTICE

A friend who helps me grow

My new school is bigger than my old one, so I felt a little nervous on the first day. Then I met Daniel. He is friendly and patient. He is good at maths, and he never laughs at me when I make mistakes. When I cannot solve a problem, I turn to him for help. He explains it slowly and makes sure I understand.

We have different hobbies. I enjoy reading stories, but Daniel loves playing the guitar. After school, we sometimes share our favourite books and songs. He is also honest. If I do something wrong, he tells me the truth but never makes me feel bad.

Daniel has helped me become more confident. I have learned that a good friend does not have to be exactly like you. A true friend respects you, supports you and helps you become a better person.

READING 2 · ORIGINAL PRACTICE

What makes a good friend?

Our class did a small survey about friendship. We asked twenty students to choose the three most important qualities of a good friend. Honesty was the most popular answer. Many students said they wanted friends they could trust. Helpfulness and kindness were also important. Some students chose humour because a funny friend can make a difficult day better.

The survey taught us that people may have different ideas about friendship. However, most of us agree on one thing: friendship needs care and respect. We should listen to our friends, keep their secrets and be there when they need us. Friendship is not only about having fun together. It is also about understanding and supporting each other.

阅读策略：理解人称代词指代

代词通常指向前文已经出现的人或事物。阅读时不要只看代词附近的一个词，要结合语法和上下文判断。

Li Hua is helpful and patient. He is also good at maths.
这里的 He 指 Li Hua。

检查方法：把代词替换成候选名词，读一遍句子，看意思和语法是否合理。

本单元辨析四个元音音素和两个辅音音素。

音标教材示例发音提示

/ɪ/his, thin短元音，发音较短促，舌位略低于 /iː/。
